How answering works

  1. Choose your answer with one hand: hold up 1 to 4 fingers for A to D.
  2. Confirm with your other hand: make a thumbs-up (closed fist, thumb pointing up).
  3. Hold both signs steady for 1.2 seconds. A white bar fills the answer, then it locks in. You can't change it afterwards.

  • Keep both hands in view of the camera, palms facing it, with good lighting.
  • Tuck your thumb in when showing fingers, and curl all fingers for the thumbs-up.
  • Questions with only two or three answers accept only those letters.
  • Each question allows one answer. The next question resets everything.
